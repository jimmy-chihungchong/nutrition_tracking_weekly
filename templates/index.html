How it works

  1. Describe a meal — foods and amounts.
  2. Calculate — an AI nutrition service estimates the nutrients in it.
  3. Review — remove anything misread or adjust amounts.
  4. Save — the meal is added to your Excel log.
  5. Track — see each day and your week against your goals.
  6. Improve — get suggestions for your biggest gaps.
